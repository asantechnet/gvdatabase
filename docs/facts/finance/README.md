# Finance, receipts and commissions

Current behavior reviewed 2026-11-11. Source owners are the income/expense controllers, `IncomeReceiptService`, `ApplicantContractPaymentService`, `CurrencyRateService`, `CommissionService`, and the finance routes in `routes/api.php`.

## Ledger model

| Record | Table / model | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income | `incomes` / `Income` | Customer receipt against an optional contracted service line. Approved rows are ledger money. |
| Expense | `expenses` / `Expense` | Company spend, optionally tied to an applicant or a payroll record. Approved rows are ledger money. |
| Finance type | `finance_types` / `FinanceType` | Editable income/expense catalogue values used by the forms and validation. |
| Currency | `currencies`, `currency_rate_history` | Currency catalogue, base currency, rate and decimal precision. |
| Commission accrual | `commission_accruals` | Earned commission created from a completed case or a completed Academy class. |
| Commission settlement | `commission_settlements` | Draft → approved → paid payout batch; paid settlement books an expense. |
| Salary expense | `expenses.salary_id` | Cash record generated by payroll; not editable/deletable through general expenses. |

`Income` and `Expense` both have `draft`, `approved`, and `rejected` status. The model defaults a directly created row to `approved`; an explicit draft stays out of the ledger until a finance approver accepts it. Only approved rows count as ledger money.

## Draft, approval, and rejection

- Creating an explicit draft requires `create_draft_finance_records`. Creating an approved record requires the matching `create_incomes` or `create_expenses` grant.
- Only a draft can be approved or rejected. `PATCH /incomes/{income}/approve|reject` and the equivalent expense routes require `approve_draft_finance_records`.
- Approval records `approved_by` and `approved_at`; rejection requires a reason and records `rejected_at` and `rejection_reason`.
- A rejected record cannot be approved later; create a new record.
- The approver is notified of a submitted draft (`financeDraftSubmitted`). The recorder is notified when the draft is approved or rejected (`financeDraftStatusChanged`).
- `view_own_finance_records` scopes lists to the recorder. `view_all_finance_records` sees all. A legacy holder of `view_incomes`/`view_expenses` without either scope keeps the historical all-records behavior. The controller enforces the same rule for show, edit, delete, receipt, and document access.
- Draft rows may be edited/deleted by their recorder with `create_draft_finance_records`; approved rows require `edit_incomes`/`edit_expenses` or `delete_incomes`/`delete_expenses` as applicable.

## Incomes and receipts

- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /incomes` use the finance view/create/edit/delete grants plus the draft grants.
- `GET /incomes/{income}/receipt` requires a view grant and only works for an approved income.
- An income may target one `applicant_contract` service line. When it does, `applicant_id` and the linked student are derived from the line, and the receipt currency must match the line currency.
- A standalone income remains valid: applicant, student, visitor, or employee may be the payer, or the receipt may be independent.
- A receipt may not exceed the line's remaining payable balance. The check converts the receipt into the line's currency using the receipt rate and line rate. Editing or re-linking a receipt re-checks the old and new lines under a lock.
- Payment state on the service line is derived by `ApplicantContractPaymentService`:
  - `payable_total = max(0, fee − discount)`.
  - `amount_paid` is the sum of approved incomes linked to the line, converted to the line currency.
  - `payment_status` is derived from paid vs payable.
  - `amount_paid_overridden` and `payment_status_overridden` pin the automatic value. `PUT /incomes/applicant-contracts/{line}/payment-override` requires `view_incomes` and `override_contract_payments`.
- Overrides change the collectible balance, never the receipt ledger. Removing an override resumes the receipt-derived value.
- A receipt number is issued only at approval, in the shape `RCPT-{year}-{000123}`. Drafts do not consume a number.
- Editing an approved income's amount, quantity, currency, or rate is a revaluation and requires a non-empty `revaluation_reason`. Description, type, and date edits do not.
- Receipt output is A5 with company branding, applicant/student identity, the contracted service line (fee, discount, paid, remaining), serial number and signature. The shared print workflow is described in [Printing](../printing.md).

### Currency and rate snapshots

- `CurrencyRateService::remember()` is the single write path for a user-supplied rate. It locks the currency, stores the positive rate (base currency is forced to 1) and returns the stored value; invalid/zero rates are ignored rather than written.
- Every income/expense stores a currency snapshot (`cnv_curr_shrt`, `base_curr_shrt`, `rate`) and `base_amount = amount × quantity ÷ rate`. Changing amount/quantity/rate recalculates `base_amount`.
- The base currency is the one with `is_base=true`; a missing base currency blocks bonus valuation and some aggregates.

## Expenses

- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /expenses` use the matching expense grants plus draft grants.
- An applicant-specific draft requires the applicant to have at least one active, non-cancelled contract (`ActiveContractService`). This is the current version of the old policy that case expenses require a live engagement.
- Expense documents are uploaded to local storage, downloaded through a permission-checked endpoint, and removed with the expense. The edit/delete document routes use `edit_expenses`/`view_expenses`.
- An expense with `salary_id` is payroll-owned. The general expense controller refuses to edit or delete it; payroll owns the cash record.
- Finance types validate against `finance_types.value` for the matching `kind` (`income` or `expense`).

## Commissions

Commission is a separate ledger from receipts.

- **Case commission:** a case-bearing contract line earns when the applicant case reaches the terminal `completed` stage. Sellers and every responsible employee on the case are eligible, but only employees marked `is_commission_based` with a positive per-service percentage earn. The eligible base is `fee − discount`, converted to base currency.
- **Academy exception:** Academy contract lines never earn counselor commission. Only a completed Academy class earns teacher commission, and only for teacher-position employees with an Academy percentage (see [Academy](../academy/README.md)).
- **Voiding:** a cancelled/terminated contract or a case that leaves `completed` voids unsettled accruals. Money attached to a settlement is immutable and is never reversed by a case/contract change.
- Accruals are keyed by contract line and employee (or Academy class and employee), so reconciliation updates amounts instead of duplicating them.
- `GET /commissions/accruals` and `/summary` use `view_commissions`. Adjustments and syncs require `manage_commission_settlements`.
- `commission-settlements` move through `draft` → `approved` → `paid`; approval uses `approve_commissions`, payment uses `pay_commissions`, and cancel/adjustment uses `manage_commission_settlements`.
- A paid settlement books an expense. The settlement slip is printable.
- The former invoice-driven commission and payment-request paths are retired. Do not reintroduce invoice/counterparty/commission-ledger compatibility endpoints.

## Payroll-related finance

- Salary payments and advances create/synchronize expenses so the cash ledger reflects payroll.
- Salary recovery of an advance is a deduction, not a second expense. A cash repayment by the employee shrinks the linked payroll expense; see [HR](../hr/README.md).
- Salary-linked expenses are managed by payroll and are read-only through general expense endpoints.

## Reports, dashboards, and analytics

- `GET /reports/catalog` and `GET /reports/{type}/export` are available to permitted report viewers; the controller applies type-specific authorization for application, finance, and HR reports.
- `GET /reports/expenses`, `/incomes`, `/salaries` and `/visitors` are legacy report aliases with their own permissions. `view_financial_reports` covers financial reports; `view_salaries` covers payroll reports.
- `/incomes`, `/expenses`, `/commissions` are the finance pages. `/finance` and the reports pages provide the broader workspace.
- `daily_analytics`/`monthly_analytics` are built by `analytics:sync`. Dashboard code falls back to source tables when aggregate coverage is incomplete.
- **Known limit:** `analytics:sync` currently sums all income/expense rows by date without filtering to `approved`. Live finance screens use the approved-only ledger. Do not rely on archived `income_total`/`expense_total` for ledger reconciliation until the sync is aligned; see [Schedules](../schedules/README.md).

## Permissions

- View/create/edit/delete incomes and expenses: `view_incomes`, `create_incomes`, `edit_incomes`, `delete_incomes`, `view_expenses`, `create_expenses`, `edit_expenses`, `delete_expenses`.
- Draft workflow: `create_draft_finance_records`, `approve_draft_finance_records`, `view_own_finance_records`, `view_all_finance_records`.
- Payment override: `override_contract_payments`.
- Currencies: `view_currencies`, `manage_currencies`.
- Commissions: `view_commissions`, `manage_commission_settlements`, `approve_commissions`, `pay_commissions`.
- Reports: `view_financial_reports`, `view_salaries`; finance types are edited with `edit_settings`.
